Lantern

Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 September 2026

Lantern is a Bible reading app. It keeps what you write and what you read on your own phone. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no server holding your reading history.

The short version

We don't collect your personal information. Your notes, your reading progress and your streak never leave your device. We have no way to read them, and no way to give them to anyone else.

What stays on your device

All of this is stored locally on your phone and is never sent to us:

  • Your notes and notebooks
  • Which reading plan you're on and which days you've finished
  • Your streak, hearts, oil, quests and ranks
  • Your answers to the questions asked when you first opened the app
  • Your settings, including your daily reminder time

If you delete Lantern from your phone, all of it is deleted with it. Because there is no cloud backup, anything you have written is gone and cannot be recovered by us or by you. If your notes matter to you, copy them somewhere else before deleting the app or changing phones.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email address or phone number
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools, and we don't profile you
  • We don't show advertising
  • We don't sell, rent or share your information, because we don't have it
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites

Reminders

If you turn on a daily reminder, the notification is scheduled by your phone and delivered by your phone. Nothing about it reaches us, and no reminder is sent from a server.

Subscriptions and payments

Lantern is a paid app with a free trial. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your card details, your billing address, or your Apple ID.

To know whether your subscription is active, the app checks with Apple's servers and with RevenueCat, the service that manages subscriptions for us. That check involves an anonymous identifier created on your device, along with which subscription you hold and when it renews. It is not linked to your name and is not used to advertise to you. RevenueCat's own privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Bible text

The Bible translations in Lantern ship inside the app and are read from your device. Looking up a passage does not tell us, or anyone else, what you read.

Children

Lantern is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for a parent to request or delete beyond deleting the app itself.

Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you the right to see, correct, export or delete the personal information a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to show or delete. The data on your phone is yours, and removing the app removes it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. If a change ever means Lantern starts collecting something it doesn't collect today, that will be said plainly in the app before it happens.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to lanternbible@gmail.com.
