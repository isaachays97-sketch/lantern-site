Support

Questions, problems, or anything that looks wrong

Get in touch

Email lanternbible@gmail.com. It's a small operation, so replies usually take a day or two.

If something's broken, it helps to say which iPhone you have, which version of iOS, and what you were doing when it happened.

Your free trial and subscription

How the trial works. Lantern is free for your first seven days. If you cancel before the trial ends you are not charged. If you don't, it continues at the price shown when you signed up.

How to cancel. Cancelling is done through Apple, not through us — we can't cancel it for you:

  • Open Settings on your iPhone
  • Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions
  • Tap Lantern, then Cancel Subscription

You keep everything until the end of the period you've already paid for.

Switching between Base and Plus. Same place — tap Lantern under Subscriptions and choose the other plan. Apple works out the difference in price for you.

New phone, or reinstalled the app. Open Lantern, and on the subscribe screen tap Restore purchase. As long as you're signed in with the same Apple ID, your subscription comes back. You will not be charged twice.

Refunds. Apple handles all refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase. We have no way to issue one directly.

Your reading

Your notes live on your phone. Lantern has no account and no cloud backup, which means nobody can read your notes but you. It also means that if you delete the app, your notes go with it, and they cannot be recovered. Copy anything you'd hate to lose before deleting the app or moving to a new phone.

Missed a day. A streak freeze protects one missed day. You start with one, and Plus holds three. A freeze protects the streak — it doesn't mark the day as read, so your plan still waits for you where you actually got to.

Out of hearts. Hearts come back on their own twelve hours after the last one goes, at no cost. You can spend oil to refill them sooner, and Plus has unlimited hearts, so wrong answers never stop you.

Reminders aren't arriving. Check Settings → Notifications → Lantern and make sure notifications are allowed. If Focus or Do Not Disturb is on at your reminder time, iOS will hold the notification back.

Changing your plan. Open the Learn tab and tap the plan name at the top. Switching plans doesn't lose the progress you made on the old one.

Found something wrong in the text?

The quiz questions and reflections were written against the Bible text itself, but mistakes are possible. If a question looks wrong, email the plan name and the day number and it will be checked and fixed.
