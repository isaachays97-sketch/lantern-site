Lantern

Read the Bible, a day at a time.

Coming to the App Store

Lantern gives you one short reading a day and asks a couple of questions afterwards — not about what it means, but about what it said. Keep the streak, and the habit builds itself.

What's in it

  • Nine reading plans, from Mark in 20 days to Psalms across 60
  • Questions written against the text, for every day of every plan
  • A streak that's forgiving, with freezes for the days life gets in the way
  • Notes you can attach to any verse
  • A daily reminder at a time you choose

Everything stays on your phone. No account, no sign-in, nothing collected.
